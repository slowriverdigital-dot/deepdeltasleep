Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026 · Last Updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Slow River Digital LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), the operator of Deep Delta Sleep, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and interact with our services, including our TikTok integration via the TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Who We Are

Deep Delta Sleep is a sleep and relaxation content brand operated by Slow River Digital LLC, based in Westport, Connecticut, USA. We publish 4K nature soundscape videos to YouTube and TikTok to help users achieve restful sleep.

Contact: slowriverdigital@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize our app through TikTok's OAuth login flow, we may collect the following information:

  • TikTok User ID: A unique identifier for your TikTok account, used to authenticate posting permissions.
  • Display name and profile information: Basic account information provided by TikTok's Login Kit (user.info.basic, user.info.profile).
  • Account statistics: Follower counts and engagement metrics available via user.info.stats scope, used only for creator eligibility verification.
  • Video publishing permissions: Access to post video content directly to your TikTok profile via the Content Posting API (video.publish, video.upload).

We do not collect passwords, financial information, or any sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our TikTok integration is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account and verify creator eligibility
  • Post sleep and relaxation video content to your authorized TikTok profile via the Direct Post API
  • Retrieve creator information required by TikTok's Content Posting API (e.g., privacy level settings, posting eligibility)
  • Comply with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and API usage policies

We do not use your information for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it outside of what is strictly necessary to operate this integration.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • TikTok: Data is transmitted to TikTok through their official API. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs how they handle this data.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain TikTok access tokens only for as long as necessary to perform authorized posting operations. Tokens are stored securely and are revoked when no longer needed. We do not store video content, personal profiles, or audience data beyond what is required for API operations.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke our app's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's app management settings. Upon revocation, we will no longer have access to post on your behalf. You may also contact us at slowriverdigital@gmail.com to request deletion of any stored data.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data handled through our API integration. Access tokens are stored securely and access is restricted to authorized systems only.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our content is designed for general adult audiences seeking sleep and relaxation.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
